Frame藏 Privacy Policy

帧藏 · Effective date: 2026-10-09

Contact / 联系我们

Email: magaxy@163.com

Summary / 概要

Frame藏 is designed to respect your privacy. The app runs entirely on your device and does not collect or upload any personal data.

帧藏尊重你的隐私。本应用完全在设备本地运行，不收集、不上传任何个人信息。

Information we handle / 我们处理什么信息

  • Photos you pick, ticket text you enter and the tickets you save are stored locally on your device. We do not upload them to any server.
  • No account sign-up. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Photo library access is used only when you choose a photo for a ticket or save an exported ticket to your photo library.
  • Device motion is read only while the glitter effect is shown, to render reflections in real time. It is never recorded or uploaded.

你选择的照片、填写的票面文字和保存的票根作品，全部只保存在设备本地，不会上传到任何服务器。不需要注册账号，不包含任何第三方统计、广告或追踪 SDK。照片权限仅在你选择照片制作票根、或将导出的票根保存到相册时使用。闪粉特效显示时会读取设备倾斜角度，仅用于实时渲染反光效果，不会记录或上传。

Purchases / 购买

Frame藏 Pro purchases are processed by Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The app only reads Apple’s purchase status to unlock or restore Pro features.

帧藏 Pro 购买由 Apple App Store 处理，我们不接触支付信息。应用只读取 Apple 提供的购买状态，用于解锁和恢复 Pro。

Third-party services / 第三方服务

The app uses Apple StoreKit for in-app purchases, subject to Apple’s terms and privacy policy. Opening this policy or the support page in the app requires an internet connection.

应用通过 Apple StoreKit 处理内购，受 Apple 条款约束。在应用内打开本页或支持页需要网络连接。

Deletion / 删除数据

You can delete a single ticket by long-pressing it in Mine → My Works. Deleting the app removes all local data. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple purchase; use Restore Purchases in Settings if needed.

可在「我的 → 我的作品」中长按作品删除单个作品；删除应用会清除全部本地数据，但不会取消 Apple 购买，可在「设置」中使用「恢复购买」。

Changes / 政策变更

We may update this policy as the product or law changes. The effective date at the top will be revised when we do.

我们可能根据功能或法律变化更新本政策，届时会更新文首生效日期。
